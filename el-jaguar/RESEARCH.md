## Caveats

- Yelp, Zmenu, Atly, Yahoo Local and Chef.i all block automated fetching, so **item-level menu
  prices could not be scraped**. The menu on the demo site is representative of the dishes named
  in reviews and typical cantina fare; every price is an illustrative placeholder and the site
  labels it as such. The owners should replace it with the real menu.
- The "live music on weekends" framing is based on review mentions of live entertainment and
  dancing; the actual entertainment schedule should be confirmed with the owners.
- Food photos are illustrative stock images from Wikimedia Commons, not photos of the venue.
