La Casa

The night starts on Avenue U

When the rest of Gravesend closes up, El Jaguar wakes up. We're a Mexican cantina in the old-school sense — a kitchen turning out tacos, gringas and tortas until late, a bar pouring margaritas and ice-cold cervezas, and a floor that fills up when the música starts.

No reservations, no dress code, no rush. Come for dinner at six, stay for the baile at midnight. Aquí se come, se canta y se baila.
