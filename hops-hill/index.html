The Bar

Beer geeks and everyone else

Hops Hill came up the hill from Clinton Hill and planted a second flag on Atlantic Avenue. Sixteen taps in constant rotation, weighted toward East Coast breweries, plus a cooler deep enough to lose an afternoon in.

The bartenders know the list cold and will happily walk you through it — but nobody here is precious about it. It stays quiet enough to actually talk, lively enough to feel like a Friday.
