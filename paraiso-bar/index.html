The House

A corner of paradise
on Bath Avenue

“I love how chill and fun the bar feels.”

Paraíso is a neighbourhood bar in the old sense — the kind of room where the bartender knows the regulars and nobody is hurrying you out. Bath Beach has had a bar on this corner for years; this is the one that stuck.

The drinks are honest and the pours are generous. There is live music most weekends, the game on when there is a game, and a kitchen next door when the night runs long. Come for one, stay for the set.

The Nights

Music, games & the room

Live Music

Most weekends the corner fills up and the band sets up. The music here always keeps the vibe alive — that is a guest’s line, not ours, and it is the reason the room works.

Game Nights

The game goes on when there is a game worth watching. Sport nights in a safe, easy atmosphere — no cover, no fuss, just a good seat and a cold one.

Private Events

Birthdays, work parties, whatever the occasion. The room is available for private bookings — call the bar and ask for dates.
